TITLE: Adil Ibrahim — CSE professor reviews
SOURCE: Rate My Professors
URL: https://www.ratemyprofessors.com/professor/3035318

OVERALL: Adil Ibrahim, Computer Science, The Ohio State University. Overall rating 2.0/5, 28% would take again, difficulty 3.8/5. Reviews are mostly for CSE 2431 (Systems II / Operating Systems) and CSE 1223. Reviews are mixed and have improved over time.

REVIEW | CSE 2431 | May 2026 | Quality 5/5 | Difficulty 2/5 | Grade A | Would take again: Yes
Honestly a pretty great professor. His deadlines are always flexible, he's pretty reachable, and his exam is quite easy. Not a lot of homeworks but he does try to give you extra practice for all the exams. The labs are tough but understandable and will help on the exams.

REVIEW | CSE 2431 | May 2026 | Quality 4/5 | Difficulty 3/5 | Grade A- | Would take again: Yes
Good professor, works to make concepts digestible. There aren't too many homework assignments. The labs can be fairly difficult, but he's helpful if you're struggling. Exams aren't too hard; he gives a good overview of what will be on them, and there are quizzes assigned prior that are similar.

REVIEW | CSE 2431 | December 2025 | Quality 2/5 | Difficulty 4/5 | Grade C
Dr. Ibrahim is a rather dry lecturer and, in my opinion, gives very challenging and unfair exams. He listens to feedback, and we got a curve for the exams, but his class felt a lot harder than it needed to be.

REVIEW | CSE 1223 | December 2025 | Quality 2/5 | Difficulty 4/5 | Grade A+
His class was very difficult. He would never respond to my emails so I had to rely on the TA. Attendance was 10% and was mandatory for labs and lecture. If you read the textbook, both were not helpful. I did like that the grade breakdown wasn't weighted heavily on one project or exam though.

REVIEW | CSE 2431 | November 2025 | Quality 3/5 | Difficulty 2/5 | Grade A | Would take again: Yes
Adil has certainly improved from what past reviews have said. Echoing others: he is very kind, caring, and accommodating and truly wants all of his students to do well. He is VERY hard to reach outside of class, but always makes time after class to answer questions. Exams are hard, but he curved and gave us extra credit, and is very open to feedback as well.

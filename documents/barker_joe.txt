REVIEW | CSE 3521 | April 2026 | Quality 3/5 | Difficulty 3/5 | Tags: Participation matters, Clear grading criteria, Respected
Joe Barker is a chill and fair professor. His exams are very fair, though almost no preparation material is given for them, and he doesn't provide much information during review days. The lectures are alright, and attendance is taken every day even though it does nothing for you. Better than the other AI professors.

REVIEW | CSE 3521 | January 2026 | Quality 2/5 | Difficulty 2/5
Similar to the previous comment, he's kind of odd. He gives the vibe of a Discord moderator. His lectures are very dry, and he reads right off the slides. He's strangely defensive when questions are asked, but at least his exams are fair and easy if you read the slides.

REVIEW | CSE 3521 | December 2024 | Quality 3/5 | Difficulty 3/5 | Tags: Lecture heavy, Graded by few things

REVIEW | CSE 3521 | December 2024 | Quality 4/5 | Difficulty 3/5 | Tags: Participation matters, Clear grading criteria, Gives good feedback

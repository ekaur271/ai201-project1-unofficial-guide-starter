OVERALL: Mark Jackson, Computer Science, The Ohio State University. Overall rating 3.3/5, 69% would take again, difficulty 2.3/5. Reviews are for CSE 2111 (Modeling/spreadsheets and databases, Excel and Access). Widely described as an easy class with a fast-paced, boring lecture style.

REVIEW | CSE 2111 | April 2026 | Quality 5/5 | Difficulty 1/5 | Grade A | Would take again: Yes
Easy A. Prelabs take time but are super easy if you know basics of Excel. Exams are multiple choice but with like 5-8 answer options. I attended only the first lab and about 5 lectures. His lectures are pretty short, most of the time taking 40 mins instead of an hour. Use lab rooms with TAs if you have any HW questions.

REVIEW | CSE 2111 | March 2026 | Quality 4/5 | Difficulty 1/5 | Grade A- | Would take again: Yes

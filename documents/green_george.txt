TITLE: George Green — CSE professor reviews
SOURCE: Rate My Professors
URL: https://www.ratemyprofessors.com/professor/2012390

OVERALL: George Green, Computer Science, The Ohio State University. Overall rating 4.0/5, 82% would take again, difficulty 2.7/5. Reviews span CSE 2431 (Systems II), CSE 3430, and CSE 2421 (Systems I). Consistently described as caring, with slow or boring lectures but very helpful review sheets and generous curves.

REVIEW | CSE 2431 | December 2025 | Quality 5/5 | Difficulty 2/5
Very caring professor that makes sure students succeed. Lectures are a little tedious and attendance is required. The exam review sheet is very helpful and the class slides are posted. Exams can be tricky but are all multiple choice and everyone did relatively well. Would highly recommend for systems.

REVIEW | CSE 2431 | December 2025 | Quality 5/5 | Difficulty 1/5 | Tags: Caring, Respected, Accessible outside class
Professor Green is an awesome guy who cares about making students succeed. He gives out review sheets that cover everything on the exams, so if you go through them, you will do well, even if you got lost in lecture. In addition, his Zoom review sessions are super helpful too. Overall a caring and great professor — if you have him, you will be fine.

REVIEW | CSE 3430 | December 2025 | Quality 3/5 | Difficulty 3/5 | Tags: Caring, Lecture heavy
Lectures are very boring but if you can engage with the content you should get an A. He makes his expectations and criteria for success very clear and knows what he's talking about. Answers questions thoroughly, appreciates participation. Attendance is taken every day but only weighted 5%. He curves all the exams to an 87 average which was awesome.

REVIEW | CSE 2421 | July 2025 | Quality 5/5 | Difficulty 2/5 | Tags: Participation matters, Caring, Graded by few things
Answered questions very well and explained the concepts clearly. The lectures are slow-paced but cover most of the details needed for the test. The professor also provides a summary document with all the potential questions for the midterm and final exams, and everything on the tests comes directly from that. Overall, a great professor!

REVIEW | CSE 2421 | July 2025 | Quality 5/5 | Difficulty 3/5 | Tags: Caring, Graded by few things, Accessible outside class
Dr. Green clearly cares about the success of his students. While the lectures can feel a bit slow at times, you always leave feeling comfortable with the topics covered. While projects are tough, Dr. Green grades generously. The exams are relatively easy, particularly if you are familiar with the content covered in lecture.
